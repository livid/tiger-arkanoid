Arkanoid

A fan remake of Arkanoid in the style of the Super Nintendo game, for Mac OS X 10.4 on PowerPC Macs: 32
rounds, then DOH. For PowerPC Macs with Mac OS X 10.4 or later; built and tested on 10.4.11.

1. Drag Arkanoid to your Applications folder and open it. The window takes the largest of 3, 2 or 1 times
   the game's 256 by 224 pixels that fits your screen. Drag its corner to resize it; it keeps its shape.

2. Return or a click starts. On the title screen, the arrow keys choose the round to start on.

3. Move the Vaus with the mouse or the left and right arrows. Space, Z, X or a click launches the ball and
   fires the laser. P pauses, Esc goes back to the title screen. Command-Q quits.

4. Catch a falling capsule with the Vaus:
     S  Slow        slows the ball
     C  Catch       the ball sticks to the Vaus until you fire
     L  Laser       the Vaus shoots while you hold fire
     E  Enlarge     the Vaus grows
     D  Disruption  the ball splits into three
     B  Break       opens a gate in the right wall to the next round
     P  Player      an extra Vaus

5. You start with 2 spare lives, get one more at 20,000 points and one every 60,000 after that. After the
   game is over you can continue on the same round. The high score is kept in your Preferences folder
   (local.exe.arkanoid.plist).

Arkanoid is a trademark of Taito. This remake has no code, pictures or sound from Taito's games: the code
draws every picture and makes every sound. Source: https://github.com/livid/tiger-arkanoid
